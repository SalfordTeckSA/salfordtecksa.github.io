SALFORDTECK WEBSITE
===================

CONTENTS
- index.html: website pages and content
- styles.css: white, blue and orange styling; responsive mobile layout
- script.js: product catalogue, category filters and quote email form

HOW TO OPEN
1. Extract the ZIP folder.
2. Open the extracted "salfordteck-website" folder.
3. Double-click index.html. It will open in your web browser.
4. Click "View All Products" to see the full sample catalogue.
5. Select a category to filter the catalogue.
6. Submit the quote form. The visitor's default email application opens with an email addressed to salfordteck@gmail.com. The visitor must press Send.

IMPORTANT CUSTOMISATIONS BEFORE PUBLISHING
1. Open index.html in Notepad or VS Code.
2. Search for "Phone 1: Add number" and replace it with the first contact's real phone number.
3. Search for "Phone 2: Add number" and replace it with the second contact's real phone number.
4. Also update the two tel:+27000000001 and tel:+27000000002 links to the real numbers in international format, e.g. tel:+27123456789.
5. Replace the sample catalogue items with the exact products, brands, prices and stock you sell.
6. Add your business address, trading details and delivery/returns information before going live.

QUOTE FORM NOTE
The quote form uses a mailto link. It does not send email silently or store enquiries on a server. It opens the customer's configured email app with the message prepared. If the visitor has no email app configured, the form may not open correctly. For reliable website-based submissions, connect a backend or a form service and configure it to deliver to salfordteck@gmail.com.

PRODUCT NOTE
The 20 products currently shown are sample catalogue entries, not confirmed stock or pricing. No prices are displayed so customers can request a quotation.
